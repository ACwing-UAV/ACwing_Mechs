# Battery trays A1

Screw-on battery trays for the frame A1 core (PETG), source meshes in the assembly position.

| File | Pack (1S LiPo) | Mass (solid) |
|---|---|---|
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T400.stl` | 400 mAh, 20 × 40 × 8 mm | 3.30 g |
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T800.stl` | 800 mAh, 25 × 45 × 9 mm | 3.55 g |
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T1200.stl` | 1200 mAh, 30 × 64 × 8 mm | 4.04 g |

- Each tray:
  - fixes to the 4 core lugs with M2 × 5 screws from below;
  - carries the Flow rev A board (2 M2 inserts, sensor side down), turned 90° with its connectors toward +X: bosses at
    (6.6, ±8), plate to y −11.6 (0.9 mm under the latch tongue), window x −10…3.2 (2026-11-08);
  - takes the pack from the rear, with a snap latch.
- All trays have the same outer rails (x ±15.3…16.5), so the tabs to the lugs are short. Narrower packs are centred by ribs.
- Print upright, flow bosses on the bed. Print files: `00 - Docs/00 - Mech_A1_Docs/Prints/`.

Flow board 1.0 mm (2026-11-08): M2 × 4 nylon screws (3.0 mm in the insert); lens 11.3 mm above the ground (T400, T1200),
10.3 mm (T800).
