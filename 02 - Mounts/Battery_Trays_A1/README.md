# Battery + flow trays A1 (rev M)

Screw-on trays (PETG) that carry the battery **and** house the Flow rev A board (rev M, 2026-11-15: they replace the
A1 trays and the flow board cover). Source meshes in the assembly position.

| File | Pack (1S LiPo) | Mass (solid) | First frame mode (FE) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T400.stl` | 400 mAh, 20 × 40 × 8 mm | 3.42 g | 85.9 Hz |
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T800.stl` | 800 mAh, 25 × 45 × 9 mm | 3.82 g | 85.6 Hz |
| `ACwing_Tray_A1_T1200.stl` | 1200 mAh, 30 × 64 × 8 mm | 4.39 g | 91.1 Hz |

- **Battery box**, open toward the deck and closed at both ends: the pack is dropped in while the tray is off the frame
  and is held between the box floor (2.4 mm strips) and the deck; put 0.5 mm foam on top. Notch in the rear wall for the
  pack leads and cable D. To change the pack, unscrew the tray.
- **Fixing:** 4 posts (Ø6, z −9.3 … −6.2) under the body pads, **M2 × 6** (alloy steel 10.9 / 12.9) from below into the hot-melt inserts in the body pads.
- **Flow pocket** under the box: the board **slides in from +X** (connector edge last), sensor side down, back flat on the
  two floor bosses (frame 6.6, ±8.0; M2 hot-melt inserts), clamped by **2 × M2 × 6 nylon** from below through the pocket
  floor (counterbores Ø4.0 × 1.4). Pocket floor 3.3 mm under the sensor face; openings only for the PMW3901 lens (Ø6,
  45° outward flare), the VL53L1X window (3.0 × 5.4) and the status LED (Ø1.2). The connector edge (+X) is open for the
  J1 / J2 plugs. The optical cones clear the openings by 0.62 mm.
- **Print upside down** (wall tops, lugs and posts on the bed), no supports: the floor strips (21.6 / 26.6 / 31.6 mm) and
  the pocket floor (22.7 mm) are bridges. Black or grey PETG. Print files: `00 - Docs/00 - Mech_A1_Docs/Prints/`.
- Ground clearance of the pocket floor with the drone landed (cup feet at z −30): 5.6 mm (T400, T1200), 4.6 mm (T800).
- Generated by `tray_M.py` (kept with the design files).
