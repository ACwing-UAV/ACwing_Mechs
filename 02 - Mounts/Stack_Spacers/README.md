# Stack spacers

`ACwing_Stack_Spacers_A1_S1x4_S2x4.stl` is one plate with 8 spacers, OD 4.5 / ID 2.3 mm, PETG, printed flat at 100 % infill.
Heights are for **1.6 mm boards** and silicone grommets H 6.0 with a 1.6 mm groove (2026-11-06):
- **S1, 2.8 mm** (×4): from the ESC 1S top (z −0.4) to the lower grommet of the FC mount (z 2.4);
- **S2, 3.8 mm** (×4): from the upper grommet (z 8.4) to the GPS rev A bottom (z 12.2), GPS option only.

With 1.0 mm boards and the old damping balls they were 2.57 / 3.57 mm.
