# Stack spacers

`ACwing_Stack_Spacers_A1_S0x4_S1x4_S2x4.stl` is one plate with 12 spacers, OD 4.5 / ID 2.3 mm, PETG, printed flat at
100 % infill. Heights for **1.0 mm boards** and the QUARKZMAN M2 damping balls (H 6.0, 2.43 mm above / below the board):
- **S0, 4.00 mm** (×4, rev M 2026-11-15): from the body boss top (z −1.5) to the ESC 1S bottom (z 2.5). The stack sits
  4.5 mm higher than in rev R so that the ESC USB-C stays free (handoff §15);
- **S1, 2.57 mm** (×4): from the ESC 1S top (z 3.5) to the lower ball of the FC mount (z 6.07);
- **S2, 3.57 mm** (×4): from the upper ball (z 11.93) to the GPS rev A bottom (z 15.5), GPS option only.
